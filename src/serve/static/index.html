What this V0 is known to get wrong

  1. Nearest-branch assignment is false. Real choice depends on travel time, malls, parking, habit, price and brand.
  2. Community centroids are not where people live. Large communities get collapsed to a point.
  3. No competitors. A branch with five rival salons next door looks identical to one with none.
  4. Female population is a poor demand proxy; the skew is spatially concentrated and doesn't capture income, age, or resident-vs-tourist mix.
  5. No revenue, footfall, staffing or lease data, so SHRINK cannot distinguish a badly-located branch from a badly-run one.
  6. The LLM classifies without ground truth. Agreement with the rubric is a sanity check, not validation.
  7. Prices are a thin basket and may not be current.
  8. Dubai only.
